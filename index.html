Read the estimate carefully

Why show a range?

A prompt does not go to a known building. The model, data-center cooling system, local weather, and electricity supply can all change the result. A single confident number would hide those unknowns.

01

One response is not a token count

This calculator counts generated replies. It does not measure tokens, prompt length, or a particular model's workload. Longer or more complex requests can require different amounts of computing.

02

Location changes the estimate

The selected examples come from Table 1 of the study. It also finds water efficiency changes over time, but these example totals do not identify the hour or actual facility used for a particular answer.

03

The lawn comparison is a scale reference

Sprinkler time assumes a 2-gallon-per-minute flow. Lawn area estimates how much space that water would cover at one inch deep. Actual sprinklers and watering practices vary.

Sources & assumptions

This calculator is a research-based illustration, not a measurement of any specific chatbot.

  1. Li, P., Yang, J., Islam, M. A. & Ren, S. (2023; v5 revised 2025). “Making AI Less ‘Thirsty’: Uncovering and Addressing the Secret Water Footprint of AI Models.” Section 3.3 assumes 0.004 kWh per medium-sized GPT-3 response. Table 1 reports total operational water per request of 7.590 mL in Texas, 16.904 mL for the U.S. average, and 47.506 mL in Washington. The calculator's 7.6–47.5 mL range compares the Texas and Washington examples shown; the paper lists other locations too. These are study estimates, not measurements of current commercial models.
  2. Lawn conversions. Sprinkler flow is set to 2 US gallons per minute as an example assumption. One US gallon spread over one square foot is about 1.604 inches deep, so one inch over one square foot is about 0.623 gallons.
Limits: These are model-based GPT-3 estimates, not a universal average for today's LLMs. Providers do not publish enough request-level operational data to verify the water used by an individual answer. The selected location is an example from the paper; it does not identify where a commercial provider ran your request.
